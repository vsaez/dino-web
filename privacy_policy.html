Privacy policy

Dino Family Assistant is a personal, non-commercial project: a household assistant that runs on a home computer for a single family. There is no company or legal entity behind it, there are no public users, and nothing is sold. This policy explains what data the assistant accesses and how that data is handled.

Who runs it

The assistant is maintained by an individual for household use. Contact: dinoklisco@gmail.com.

Data it accesses

With the explicit authorization of each account owner through Google OAuth, the assistant accesses:

  • Gmail — read-only access to messages, in order to find household matters such as utility bills or school communications.
  • Google Calendar — read-only access to events, in order to answer questions about the household schedule.

This covers a small number of Google accounts, all belonging to members of the same household who have given their consent. The assistant also processes documents handed to it directly, such as school menu PDFs, and replies to messages sent to a private Telegram bot.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the household's questions and to carry out the organization tasks described above. Processing happens on the home computer. Data is not shared with third parties, not sold, not used for advertising or profiling, and not used to train models.

Storage and security

Access tokens and any temporary data needed to operate are kept on the home computer, restricted to the person who administers it. This project keeps no copy on external servers it operates. Data is deleted once it is no longer needed, or when access is revoked.

Revoking access

Any account owner can revoke the permission at any time from their Google account permissions, or email to request deletion of data associated with their account. Once access is revoked, the assistant can no longer read that mailbox or calendar.

Changes

If the way the assistant works changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: dinoklisco@gmail.com.
