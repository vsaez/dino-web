Dino Family Assistant

Dino is a private, local-first family assistant. It runs on a home computer and helps with day-to-day household organization: reading calendar events, checking email for household matters such as utility bills, answering questions through a Telegram bot, and processing school menu PDFs. It is used by a single household and is not a public product.
